# v1.10.1 — New icon

## Changed

- **A new icon: the Claw House.** The integration now uses a claw wrapped around a house, in blue on light themes and white on dark themes. It replaces the lobster illustration, which showed as a white square on dark themes and lost its detail at small sizes.

There are no functional changes in this release.

## Upgrade

Update through HACS, then **restart Home Assistant**. If the old icon still shows afterwards, refresh your browser, since Home Assistant caches brand images.
